In quiet code, I chase a fading light,
Through sleepless nights and dreams that feel like flight.
A thousand thoughts run wild inside my head,
Like rivers rushing where my heart is led.

I build new worlds with logic, clean and bright,
And turn small sparks into a shining site.
When errors rise, I breathe and try again,
For every fall still teaches something then.

The morning comes, my hope begins to grow,
Soft as the sunrise’s gentle golden glow.
One day I’ll reach the peak I’m aiming for—
Not just to win, but to become much more.
